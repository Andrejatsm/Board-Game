#  Fantasy Board Game RPG
2.5D turn-based strategy game built in Unity. Players race to the finish line while navigating a board filled with traps, boosts.

#  About The Game
This project combines classic board game mechanics (similar to Mario Party).
Up to 4 players—either Human or AI bots—take turns rolling dice to navigate the board. Landing on specific tiles can help you, hurt you.

**Turn-Based Strategy:**
- Play against smart AI bots that roll, move, and fight automatically.
  
**Dynamic Board Tiles:**

- 🟢 Boost: Move extra steps forward.
  
- 🔴 Trap: Get pushed backward.

- ☠️ Reset: A punishment tile that sends you back to the start.

- 🏆 Win: Reach the end to claim victory.

# How to Play

**Roll the Dice:**
- Press the Roll button to determine your movement.

**Navigate:**
- Watch your character move automatically across the tiles.

**Resolve Effects:**

- If you land on a Trap or Boost, the effect happens immediately.

- If you land on a Battle, the camera shifts to the arena. Win the fight to continue; lose, and you skip your next turn!

- Win: The first player to land on the Final Tile wins the game.
